# v1.8 Invariants — Adaptive Verification Economy

## I-131 — Declared Cost Is Not Accounting Cost

A caller/work-item cost claim is provenance, not the allocator's trusted cost.

```text
DeclaredCost != AccountingCost
```

The reference allocator uses a bootstrap estimate or an observation-derived estimator instead.

## I-132 — Cost Measurement Requires Separate Authority

A cost observation is accepted only through `VERIFICATION_COST_METER` authority.

A verification submitter does not acquire accounting authority by submitting work.

## I-133 — Cost Observation Is Completion-Bound

An observed cost must reference an existing completed verification and its exact `completion_hash`.

```text
CostObservation(work) => Completed(work)
```

## I-134 — One Work, One Accounting Observation

The reference store accepts at most one cost observation per exact `work_hash`.

Conflicting remeasurement requires an explicit future correction/reconciliation protocol rather than silent overwrite.

## I-135 — Estimator Evidence Is Hash-Bound

A `CostEstimatorSnapshot` commits to the exact set of cost-observation hashes from which its sample count, total, and mean were derived.

## I-136 — Unknown Cost Uses Conservative Bootstrap

No cost history does not mean zero cost and does not mean the submitter's declared cost.

```text
NoHistory -> BootstrapCost + UncertaintyPremium
```

under the reference policy.

## I-137 — Low-Sample Estimates Carry Explicit Uncertainty Premium

Until the configured minimum sample count is reached, observed mean cost is increased by the configured uncertainty premium.

## I-138 — Historical Cost May Reorder Future Attention

Authenticated observations may change future estimated costs and therefore the order in which finite verification budget is allocated.

This adaptation changes attention allocation, not truth.

## I-139 — Funding Is Not Verification

A funded candidate has received budget only.

```text
FUNDED != VERIFIED
```

The v1.7 completion/finalization plane remains authoritative for verification state.

## I-140 — Funding Is Not Semantic Truth

```text
FUNDED != PASS
DEFERRED != FAIL
```

Economic disposition cannot rewrite A3/A4 semantic decisions.

## I-141 — Budget Disposition Completeness

Every offered economic candidate occurs exactly once in:

```text
FUNDED
DEFERRED_BUDGET
DEFERRED_OVERSIZED
```

with pairwise-disjoint membership.

## I-142 — Economic Aging Preserves Competition for Old Debt

Waiting time increases reference utility through a deterministic aging term so continuously arriving fresh work cannot keep every old candidate permanently at the same priority score.

This does not imply that work larger than the entire budget can fit.

## I-143 — Oversized Does Not Mean Invalid

A candidate whose estimated cost exceeds the current whole budget is explicitly `DEFERRED_OVERSIZED`.

```text
TooExpensiveNow != Invalid
```

## I-144 — Budget Finalization Requires Keeper Authority

Only a proof with exact `VERIFICATION_BUDGET_KEEPER` role and global economy scope may finalize an allocation in the reference runtime.

## I-145 — Budget Finalization Is State-Fresh

Finalization binds the exact current economy state, policy, allocation, generation, and runtime time.

If candidates or cost-estimator evidence changed after preview:

```text
OldPreview != CurrentBudgetAuthority
```

and the commit is rejected.

---

## Boundary statement

v1.8 does not claim that `observed_cost_units` are automatically derived from physical compute. The reference model authenticates who may provide the accounting measurement and binds it to completed work. Production deployments must additionally bind that meter to protected resource telemetry or billing evidence.
